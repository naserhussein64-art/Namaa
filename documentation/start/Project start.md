# Namaa Project Start

- **Document status:** Frozen starting record
- **Version:** 0.1
- **Compilation date:** 1 August 2026
- **Responsible role:** Namaa project leadership
- **Current concept:** [Project dossier](../core/Project%20dossier.md)

This short record preserves Namaa's starting orientation and the decision to continue concept development. It is intentionally frozen so that it does not become a second, competing source for the evolving project concept.

## Starting idea

Namaa began as a proposal to make educational and developmental support available to children in Syria. The project would use expertise available through YISA and its network, including educational, child-support, and technology experience, while delivery with children would be led locally by trained Syrian teachers or facilitators.

The initial concept considered school learning, health and life knowledge, inclusion, sport, coding, playful learning, and optional digital support. These were possible components, not an approved curriculum or programme package.

## Starting problem orientation

The project moved away from the claim that services are simply absent. Its retained working direction is the lack of sufficiently regular, inclusive, and measurable support pathways for some children, with limited reach of existing offers and administrative barriers treated as contributing concerns rather than universally proven causes.

The detailed problem, retained target-group frame, current decisions, and evidence limits now belong only in the [Project dossier](../core/Project%20dossier.md) and [Problem landscape and evidence](../modules/Problem%20landscape%20and%20evidence.md).

## Continuation decision

- **Decision:** Continue developing Namaa as a coherent concept and prepare a reliable basis for development by qualified specialists.
- **Reason:** The broad education and support need is credible, while programme content, pedagogy, target priority, location, and implementation arrangements require specialist and later operational decisions.
- **Deciding role:** Namaa project leadership.
- **Original decision date:** Not separately recorded; this document does not invent one.
- **Current next decision:** Defined in the [Project dossier](../core/Project%20dossier.md).

## Boundary of this record

Jaramana was an early example and is not a selected location. A standalone local-validation workstream is not the current project phase. The initial internal software idea remains a possible enabling component, not an approved requirement.

This continuation decision does not authorise a curriculum, specialist appointment, training cohort, partner commitment, contact with children, data processing, technology build, funding application, pilot, implementation, or public claim of effectiveness.
